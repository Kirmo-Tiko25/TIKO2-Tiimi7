Introduction

Somewhere deep in a glowing nebula, a little alien called Snorp is bouncing around space in his ship. Asteroids, planets, black holes, and even a sneaky rival alien named Orlop are headed straight for him and only you can save the day!

Alien Adrift is a physics-based touch game where you don't control the alien, you control everything around him. Flick small asteroids out of the way, drag big ones with gravity, smash planets apart to collect bonus lives, and wrangle Orlop before he causes trouble. The longer you keep the chaos under control, the higher your score climbs!

Designed for everyone from little ones to older adults, Alien Adrift keeps hands busy and minds engaged. Accessible and easy to jump into, it's a natural fit for shared spaces and drop-in play. A local leaderboard adds friendly competition, and can be turned off for a more relaxed experience.

Whether you're playing solo or with a friend, there's something here for everyone. Snorp is counting on you!
