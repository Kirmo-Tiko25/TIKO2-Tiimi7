# Black hole
This threat is a blackhole that spawns from an edge and travels across the screen and consumes other hazards as they are dragged in.

## Functionality
Black holes main idea is:
- When it spawns to the map it starts to pull floating object towards it slowly. Player can drag other hazards into it.
- When the blackhole has passed through the screen for example left to right it dissappears along with the objects that it has eaten.

## Gameplay
- Blackhole affects the gameplay by giving the player a way to clear the screen making the game slightly easier as time passes.

## Map
- A blackhole works well with our space themed level and hazards.
